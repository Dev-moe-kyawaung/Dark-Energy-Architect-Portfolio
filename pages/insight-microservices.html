Microservices vs Monolith: A Practical Guide

June 2026 · 12 min read

The microservices vs monolith debate is often framed as a binary choice. In reality, it's a spectrum, and the right answer depends on your team, your product, and your stage of growth.

When to Choose a Monolith

Start with a monolith when: you have a small team (< 10 engineers), you're validating product-market fit, your deployment frequency is low, and operational complexity is a concern. Monoliths are easier to debug, test, and deploy.

When to Choose Microservices

Consider microservices when: you have multiple teams working independently, you need different scaling characteristics for different components, you're deploying multiple times per day, and you can handle the operational overhead.

Migration Strategies

The strangler fig pattern works best: gradually extract services from the monolith, starting with the least coupled components. Use feature flags, maintain backward compatibility, and migrate incrementally.

Key Lessons

  • → Start monolithic, extract when you feel the pain
  • → Service boundaries are the hardest part
  • → Distributed transactions are a last resort
  • → Observability is non-negotiable
  • → Team structure matters more than technology

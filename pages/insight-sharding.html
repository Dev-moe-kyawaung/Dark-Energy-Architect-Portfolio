Database Sharding Strategies

April 2026 · 15 min read

Horizontal scaling through sharding is one of the most powerful techniques for database performance, but it's also one of the most complex. Here's what you need to know.

Sharding Patterns

  • → Key-based: Hash function on shard key
  • → Range-based: Partition by value ranges
  • → Directory-based: Lookup service for shard mapping
  • → Geographic: Shard by region

Key Challenges

Cross-shard queries are expensive, rebalancing is complex, and choosing the right shard key is critical. Plan for hot spots and design your schema accordingly.
